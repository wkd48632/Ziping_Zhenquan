# Book 1
## Lexicology
- The Expansion: Yang of Yin-Yang
- The Convergence: Yin of Yin-Yang
- The Facilitation: Supporting force to creating or expanding spirit.
- The Containment: Regulate or attacking force to spirit
- 10-ideal-world-spirit: Called '10-gan'. Pure type of sprit. Big/small * wood/fire/earth/steel/water
- 12-real-world-sprit: Called '12-ji'. Not pure and Complicated form of pure spirit(10-ideal-world-spirit).

    
## About the 10-ideal-world-spirit and the 12-real-world-spirit
Let there was just a chaos.  
Dynamic part of chaos goes to Yang.
Static part of chaos goes to Yin.
  
Then rising and flourishing exist,  
Rising part became small Yin/Yang.  
Flourishing part became big Yin/Yang.  
We call it as '4-phase'.  
  
Big Yin goes to water.  
Big Yang goes to fire.  
Small Yang goes to wood.  
Small Yin goes to steel.  
And the harmony between them called earth.  
We call it as 5-characteristics (Wu-xing)  
  
Then each characteristics also have Yin and Yang.  
  
For example, the Big Wood is the antecedent condition of the Small Wood,  
And the Small Wood is the consequent condition of the Big Wood.  
  
Big wood germinate and expand beneath the surface of reality (invisible atmosphere).  
Expanded spirit bursts and tears out the surface of reality and acts upon real-world-things. (real-world action of spirit).  
And the Small Wood takes the vigor and the spiritual energy from real-world-things. (the cosmic energy transformed by changes in reality).  
*(Editor: And then it causes the Big Fire)*
