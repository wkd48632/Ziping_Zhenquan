The True Explanation of Ziping about oriental date fortune-telling

## You should memorize before read
I'm really sorry to say this, you have to get comfortable this fundamental symbols.

- 4 Phases
  - +: Yang, Positive, Expansion
  - -: Yin, Negative, Shrinkage, Taking back
  - ✨: Saeng[sang]: Supporting, creating, faciliting
  - ⚔️: Geuk[gk, guk]:  Containment, regulate, attacking force
    
- 10 heavenly stems:
  - 甲: +Wood, tree, expansion, beginning
  - 乙: -Wood, grass, vine or creeper, tenacious vitality
  - 丙: +Fire, The Sun, Shine brightly
  - 丁: -Fire, torch, star light, candle light
  - 戊: +Earth, wide open field, mountain
  - 己: -Earth, swamp, soil in the pot
  - 庚: +Steel, axe, raw ore
  - 辛: -Steel, knife, jewel, processed iron
  - 壬: +Water, ocean
  - 癸: -Water, raindrop

  - 子: Flourishing Water

  - 卯: Flourishing Wood
  - 辰: Dry Earth (confine water in a warehouse)
  - 巳: Bursting Fire
  - 午: Flourishing Fire
  - 未: Wet Earth (confine wood in a warehouse)
